Infrastructure doesn’t keep up.

  • !
    Reactive planning. Governments build schools after years of overcrowding — not before families arrive.
  • ?

The Solution

From raw data to
where to build next.

Xpand is an AI-powered decision intelligence platform that detects service gaps across the UAE, forecasts emerging demand, and turns each gap into an actionable proposal — connected to investors ready to fill it.

01

Detect

Joins land, population, demand & real OpenStreetMap amenities to score every area on how well its residents are served.

02

Forecast

Projects the next 12 months from price momentum and growth, then translates new residents into needed services.

03

Connect

Turns the widest gaps into open RFPs — the need, the vacant land, and the investors already interested.
